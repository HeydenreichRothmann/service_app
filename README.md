# Service App

### 🛠️ Showcase Your Skills. Connect with Clients. Grow Your Hustle.

---

## 🚀 Vision

**To create a thriving, accessible digital marketplace where anyone — from students to side hustlers to small business owners — can share their talents, reach more clients, and turn their passion into profit.**

---

## 🎯 Mission

**Hustle Hub aims to empower individuals by providing a mobile-first platform where users can easily upload, promote, and manage their services — all from their phone.**

Whether you’re a baker, tutor, handyman, hair stylist, designer, or dog walker — Hustle Hub gives you the tools to:

- Create a professional profile
- Showcase your skills with images, pricing, and descriptions
- Connect directly with nearby customers
- Build trust with ratings and reviews
- Accept bookings and payments with ease

---

## 🌟 Why Hustle Hub?

In today's world, **side hustles are becoming main hustles**. But too many talented people still rely on word-of-mouth or outdated methods to find clients. Hustle Hub bridges that gap — making it easier than ever to grow your hustle, your way.
